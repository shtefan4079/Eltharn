coi-serviceworker.js
====================

This locally modified cross-origin isolation worker follows the worker-side
implementation of coi-serviceworker by Guido Zuidhof and contributors. Its
install/activate handlers, fetch compatibility guard and response-header
reconstruction are retained with modifications for Eltharn. It is attributed
under the upstream MIT license rather than claimed as wholly original code.
