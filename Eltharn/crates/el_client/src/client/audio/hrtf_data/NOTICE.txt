Eltharn uses the complete 368 supplied 44.1 kHz, stereo 128-sample responses.
The compact symmetric dataset covers azimuth 0..180 degrees (clockwise from
front) and elevations -40..90 degrees. Opposite azimuths swap ears; elevations
below -40 degrees use the lowest measured ring. Samples are unchanged signed
16-bit PCM. The import recorded elevations/azimuths and repacked the WAV
samples without altering their response or interaural time difference.
This is Eltharn's binaural backend, not OpenAL's precise default HRTF dataset.
